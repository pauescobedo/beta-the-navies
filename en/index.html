The band

Seven musicians.
One current.

We are seven young musicians brought together by a passion for music and a desire to play, learn and enjoy the stage together. Every voice and every instrument has an equal place in The Navies.

Along the way, we have the support of BCN Fusió, who are helping us continue to grow as a band.
